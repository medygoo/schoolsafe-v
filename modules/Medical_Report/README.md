DESCRIPTION
-----------
This module provides medical reports based on the student information recorded under the _Medical_ tab.

- **Nurse Visits**: list the students who went to the nurse's office, along with date, time in/out, reason, result and comments
- **Immunizations or Physicals**: list the students who had immunizations or physicals, along with date and comments

Lists can be filtered by date, searched and exported to Excel.

A checkbox allows to "Include Inactive Students".

Translated in French, Spanish, Russian and Ukrainian.

CONTENT
-------
Students
- Medical Report

INSTALL
-------
Copy the `Medical_Report/` folder (if named `Medical_Report-main`, rename it) and its content inside the `modules/` folder of RosarioSIS.

Go to _School Setup > School Configuration > Modules_ and click "Activate".

Requires RosarioSIS 11.7+
